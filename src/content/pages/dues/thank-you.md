Thank you for contributing to the Knollwood Village Civic Club. Your payment funds the security patrol, deed restriction enforcement, the esplanades, the newsletter, and neighborhood events.

If you paid online, you'll receive an email receipt from PayPal. Your lot will show as paid on the [dues participation map](/dues/map/) once the treasurer records the payment, usually within two weeks.

Questions? Email [treasurer@knollwoodvillage.org](mailto:treasurer@knollwoodvillage.org).
