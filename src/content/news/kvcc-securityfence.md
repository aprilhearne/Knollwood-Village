<p>The Civic Club will hold a board meeting to discuss and vote on a new security measures fence policy on Wednesday, October 26th at 6p, the meeting is open to all residents. <br><br>Meeting details are also available on our website at www.knollwoodvillage.org. <br><br>The proposed policy draft (click <a href="https://kvcc.egnyte.com/dl/Aoh3eUST8h">HERE</a>) is available for your review and you are welcome to send comments / questions prior to the meeting to <a href="mailto:build@knollwoodvillage.org">build@knollwoodvillage.org</a> , which we will address.  KVCC is subject to Chapter 202 of the Texas Property Code, as are all community associations, regardless of their designation as a homeowners association, condo association or civic club. Chapter 202 requires the association adopt various policies if they intend to enforce certain provisions. This policy pertains to residents who wish to install fencing/gates in their front yard in front of the front building line for security purposes.<br> <br><em>Thank you,<br>Michelle Moudry<br>Knollwood Village Civic Club President</em></p>
