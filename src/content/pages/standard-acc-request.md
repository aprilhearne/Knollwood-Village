---
title: "Standard ACC Request"
description: "For a new fence, a storage shed, or a change to the front of the home not tied to an addition or new construction. No fee."
---

Use this process when your request is for a new fence, a storage shed, or a change to the front of the home (for example a remodel of the facade) or a related item **not** affiliated with an addition or new build. For anything that changes the size or shape of your home, use the [new construction / addition request](/new-construction-addition-acc-request/) instead.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Read the <a href="/acc-faqs/">ACC FAQs</a> and the <a href="/deeds/">deed restrictions</a> for your section.</li>
<li>Download the <a href="/docs/acc/Plan-Approval-Submission-Form.pdf">Plan Approval Submission Form</a> and complete the top half.</li>
<li>Scan the completed form and email it to <a href="mailto:build@knollwoodvillage.org">build@knollwoodvillage.org</a>. If possible, include relevant photos or a sketch.</li>
</ol>

<div class="notice">The ACC has up to 30 days to complete its initial review. You'll be notified by email if the submission is incomplete or doesn't meet the deed restrictions, and you'll receive a signed approval letter by email when your project is approved. There is no fee for a standard request.</div>

Questions? Email the ACC chair at [build@knollwoodvillage.org](mailto:build@knollwoodvillage.org).
