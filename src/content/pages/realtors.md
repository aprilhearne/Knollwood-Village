We get the same questions from closers and agents every week, so here are the answers in one place.

## The short version

**Knollwood Village Civic Club is a voluntary civic club, not a mandatory homeowners association.** Membership and dues are optional. The club does not have the authority to assess, lien, or foreclose, and no dues obligation runs with the property.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is there a mandatory HOA or property owners association? | **No.** |
| Are there mandatory dues or assessments? | **No.** Annual dues are voluntary. |
| Is a resale certificate (Texas Property Code Chapter 207) required or available? | **No.** Chapter 207 does not apply because there is no mandatory association. We do not issue resale certificates. |
| Are there transfer fees, estoppel fees, or capitalization fees? | **No.** |
| Can unpaid dues become a lien on the property? | **No.** Dues are not owed by the property or by any owner. |
| Is there a management company? | **No.** The club is run by volunteer neighbors. |
| Are there deed restrictions? | **Yes.** Every section of Knollwood Village and Braes Terrace II has recorded deed restrictions. They are enforced by the club's Architectural Control Committee. |
| Is there an architectural review requirement? | **Yes**, for construction that changes the size, shape, or exterior of a home, and for fences and sheds. See [Building and deed restrictions](/build/). |
| Is there a security patrol? | Yes, Smith Protective Services, funded by voluntary dues and shared with three neighboring civic clubs. |

## What to give your buyer

- The [deed restrictions for the property's section](/build/#sections). Type the address into the map to find the section and the PDF.
- The [New Resident Guide](/new-residents/), which covers the patrol, trash days, dues, and how to get on the email list.
- A note that the club welcomes voluntary dues of $290 a year, which fund the patrol and the rest of what the club does.

## What we cannot provide

We cannot issue resale certificates, payoff statements, estoppel letters, or "HOA questionnaires," because none of those apply to a voluntary club. If a lender's form requires an association response, this page and the [bylaws](/docs/club/KVCC-Bylaws-2004.pdf) are the documentation. Feel free to attach a printout.
