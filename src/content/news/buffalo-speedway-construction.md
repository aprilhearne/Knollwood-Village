---
title: "Buffalo Speedway Sewer Line Construction"
date: 2023-05-02
---

<p>In March 2023, our civic club learned of a construction project that is going to impact some of our residents and the neighborhood in these early summer months through at least the rest of the 2023 year.</p>

<p>It’s a project to increase the size of the sewer lines coming from NRG Park and possibly other surrounding properties. The construction / work began a few months ago. You may have noticed the construction at the corner of Main St / Murworth. It’s going to travel down Murworth then to Buffalo Speedway and along the east side of the street all the way to the bayou and eventually to the West U treatment plant on N. Braeswood near Kirby.  For more details please review the project link below.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.engagehouston.org/westridge-regional-lift-station">https://www.engagehouston.org/westridge-regional-lift-station</a></p>

<p>The city is supposed to be communicating with affected residents which is likely just those properties along Buffalo Speedway. </p>

<p>The civic club and the Braeswood Super Neighborhood has representation attending Monthly project meetings and will try to keep residents updated on any newsworthy updates.  Should a homeowner have a concern, please reach out to the project manager, Edwin Lloyd at <a href="mailto:elloyd@lsa-engineers.com">elloyd@lsa-engineers.com</a>.</p>
