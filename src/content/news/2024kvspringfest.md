---
title: "Neighborhood Spring Festival 2024"
date: 2024-03-27
---

<p>Our Social Committee is planning a <strong>Spring Festival</strong> in April so everyone please mark your calendars for this fun neighborhood event:<br><br>Saturday, April 20<br>12:00 PM - 2:00 PM<br>Bailey Park (Corner of Conway and Greenbush)<br>8011 Greenbush<br><br>The event starts at noon and is expected to include a small parade for families and children as well as Ben &amp; Jerry's and True Dog Food trucks, face painting, music, games and most importantly, hopefully lots of friendly neighbors.<br><br>Festival Details:</p>

<ul>
<li>Festival is from 11:50a to 2p</li>

<li>Families interested in doing the parade, please email Suzanne (sludlow53@gmail.com) with the name and age of your participants.</li>

<li>Parade participants celebrate Spring by wearing costumes, painting their faces, carrying signs, decorating bikes and wagons, or even playing an instrument.</li>

<li>Arrive at Bailey Park (Conway St. at Greenbush) at 11:50 AM  where we will assemble.</li>

<li>Parade watchers will line up on Conway Street.</li>

<li>Bring a folding chair if you need one.</li>

<li>Please respect the lawns.</li>

<li>Traffic will be blocked between 12-1 PM and volunteers will be on hand to guide the participants up and back along Conway St.</li>

<li>Safety: We cannot accept any motorized vehicles. SEAL Security will be present for some of the event and patrolling the neighborhood.</li>

<li>Limit bringing only friendly animals.</li>

<li>Parents please accompany the youngest children.</li>

<li>Return to Bailey Park for food, fun, and frivolity!</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Please MARK your calendars and tell your neighbors!</strong></p>

<p>Knollwood Village Civic Club<br>Social Committee<br>social@knollwoodvillage.org<br>sludlow53@gmail.com</p>
