<p>As a resident of Knollwood Village, your participation and support of the Civic Club is <strong>critical</strong> to maintaining and helping to keep our neighborhood safe.  For instance, your membership fees allow the civic club to provide a <strong>security </strong>presence, keep the medians attractive, enforce Deed Restrictions, and provide the most current news / communication to our residents. </p>

<p>Your dues will go directly to fund the following items:</p>

<ul>
<li>Neighborhood Security provided by S.E.A.L Security;</li>

<li>Legal, Fees, &amp; Insurance expenses which help protect our deed restrictions and residents;</li>

<li>Communication &amp; Social – Our web site, social events, newsletters, mailouts as well as email news alerts and</li>

<li>The beautification and maintenance of the esplanades.</li>
</ul>

<p>The reality of living in a big city is that we learn to live with crime.  However, there is something you can do to help keep our families and neighbors safe. <strong> Pay your homeowners dues!</strong>  Over sixty percent of our neighborhood expenses are for S.E.A.L. security patrols to help watch over your property. </p>

<p>Our neighborhood also has an active architectural review board that seeks to ensure all newly constructed and remodeled homes are built within the guidelines established and voted on by our residents.   </p>

<p>The other benefit of our civic club is the sense of community we feel through our sponsored social events, like the neighborhood night out, and yard decorating contests.   </p>

<p>Please take a moment to pay your 2023 dues and help us continue to protect our investment and keep our neighborhood safe, beautiful, and a place we love to call home. <strong>The total amount due is $290.00.  </strong></p>

<li>Pay online with a credit card or PayPal at <a href="/contribute/">knollwoodvillage.org/contribute/</a></li>
